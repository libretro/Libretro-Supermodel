# Contributing

Hi, and thanks for stopping by!

This core is a hobby project, kept alive by people who love these arcade games
and run them on whatever they have at home.

If it runs on your machine, you are already helping: tell us how it went,
in the Sega Model 3 thread on the Libretro Discord or in an issue here.
If it does not run, tell us that too, with your platform, your RetroArch version
and the game, and we will look at it together.

Fixed something, or built something new? Send it over as a pull request and say
what you tried it on.

Have a bigger idea? Drop a line first, in an issue or on Discord, so we can think
about it together. Fixes from upstream Supermodel are always welcome,
just as they are.

Everything here is GPLv3 or later, like Supermodel itself.

Thanks, and have fun!
